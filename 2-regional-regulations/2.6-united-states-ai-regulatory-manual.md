## 🏛️ Module 1: The US Fragmented Regulatory Architecture

### 1. The Statutory Framework & Oversight
The United States governs AI through a hybrid network of federal executive mandates and binding state-level consumer protection acts:
* **Federal Mandates:** Anchored by the comprehensive **White House Executive Order on Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy AI**, which instructs federal agencies to regulate safety, security, and algorithmic civil rights.
* **State Legal Controls:** Led by the **California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA / CCPA)**, which enforces rigid legal boundaries on automated profiling and automated decision-making technologies (ADM).
* **Enforcement Bodies:** The **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)** aggressively polices commercial systems for deceptive practices, hidden algorithmic bias, and unlawful data ingestion under **Section 5 of the FTC Act**.

### 2. The Cost of Non-Compliance & Algorithmic Disgorgement
The US enforcement mechanisms features high-stakes intellectual property and financial penalties:
* **FTC Algorithmic Disgorgement Orders:** If a company trains an AI model or fine-tunes an embedding on data collected deceptively or without permission, the FTC has the authority to issue a complete **destruction order forcing the company to delete the entire model architecture, weights, and training datasets**.
* **CPRA Civil Penalties:** California courts and the CPPA can enforce statutory penalties up to **\$7,500 per intentional violation** under the CPRA for automated processing profiling infractions.

## 🔍 Module 4: The 2 Operational US Compliance Gates

### 1. CPRA Automated Decision-Making Opt-Outs
* **The Mandate:** Consumers must be provided explicit notice and the right to opt out of the use of automated decision-making technology for profiling.
* **The Operational Fix:** Configure your application's user directory to tag profiles with an `adm_opt_out: true` flag. If flagged, route their requests to a standard traditional processing system instead of an automated model path.

### 2. FTC-Compliant Data Sourcing Checkpoints
* **The Mandate:** Ensure data used for model training or ingestion has verifiable provenance and does not contain deceptive consumer collections.
* **The Operational Fix:** Establish an immutable logging ledger tracking data licenses and explicit consent strings before data enters any machine learning training environment.
