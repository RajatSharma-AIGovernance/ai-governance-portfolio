# 2.6 - United States AI Regulatory Manual

This handbook serves as an enterprise operational reference for navigating the fragmented US artificial intelligence regulatory ecosystem. It maps federal directives and major state laws into clear corporate compliance check-points.

---

## 🏛️ Module 1: The US Fragmented Legal Landscape

### Federal vs. State Frameworks
The United States governs AI through a hybrid network of executive mandates and strict state-level consumer protections. At the federal level, compliance is shaped by the **White House Executive Order on Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy AI** and enforced via agencies like the FTC. Simultaneously, individual states enforce rigid, binding compliance boundaries—such as the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)** and strict state-level automated decision-making limits.

## 🔍 Module 3: Deep-Dive Operational Breakdown (The US Compliance Gates)

### 1. CPRA Automated Decision-Making Opt-Outs
* **What the Regulation Means:** Consumers must be provided explicit notice and the right to opt out of the use of automated decision-making technology for profiling.
* **The Real-World Fix:** Configure your application's user directory to tag profiles with an `adm_opt_out: true` flag. If flagged, route their requests to a standard traditional processing system instead of an automated model path.
