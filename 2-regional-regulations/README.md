## 🗺️ Active Jurisdictional Manuals

* 🇪🇺 **[2.1 - EU AI Act Compliance Handbook](./2.1-eu-ai-act-compliance-handbook.md)**
  * *Focus:* The European Union's 4-tier risk classification system, statutory timelines (2025–2027), and mandatory Article 9–14 compliance gates [2.1].
* 🇭🇰 **[2.2 - Hong Kong Banking AI Handbook](./2.2-hong-kong-banking-ai-handbook.md)**
  * *Focus:* High-level banking principles mapped to the HKMA core circulars and PCPD model procurement validation frameworks.
* 🇸🇬 **[2.3 - Singapore AI Governance & Financial Sector Handbook](./2.3-singapore-ai-governance-handbook.md)**

* 🇮🇳 **[2.4 - India AI Governance & DPDP Act Manual](./2.4-india-dpdp-ai-governance.md)**
  * *Focus:* Data principal rights, purpose limitations under the DPDP Act 2023, and algorithmic bias controls matching MeitY advisories.
* 🇦🇪 **[2.5 - Dubai & UAE Enterprise AI Governance Handbook](./2.5-dubai-uae-ai-governance-handbook.md)**
  * *Focus:* UAE National Strategy for AI 2031 parameters, Dubai Digital Authority ethics, and DIFC Data Protection Law procurement filters.
* 🇺🇸 **[2.6 - United States AI Regulatory Manual](./2.6-united-states-ai-regulatory-manual.md)**
  * *Focus:* Federal White House Executive Orders aligned with the NIST AI RMF, FTC fairness rules, and California CPRA consumer profiling opt-outs.
* 🇦🇺 **[2.7 - Australia AI Governance & Guardrails Guide](./2.7-australia-ai-governance-guide.md)**
  * *Focus:* National AI Centre (NAIC) voluntary safety guardrails and emerging mandatory enforcement proposals for high-risk autonomous use cases.
