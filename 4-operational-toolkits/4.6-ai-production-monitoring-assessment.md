## 🏛️ Module 1: Executive Summary & Objective

This template serves as an end-to-end operational framework for evaluating, structuring, and auditing production metrics for enterprise AI applications. To prevent subjective guesswork, this tool incorporates a **Smart Scoping Guard** (Module 2) that establishes deterministic "If/Then" logic switches. By auditing the system's runtime parameters, data types, environment, and user base, compliance leads can effortlessly calculate which monitoring controls are mandatory by default.

## 🧠 Module 2: The Automated Scoping & Applicability Logic Guide
*Use this logic guide to effortlessly fill out the "Metric Application" column in the Master Matrix below. Match your project’s architecture against these five core scoping filters:*

### 🧪 Rule 1: The Environment Filter (Production vs. Test/Staging)
* **IF your AI project is strictly in a Test, Sandbox, or Staging Environment using only synthetic/fake data:**
  * 🟢 **THEN ONLY the "Training/Run" frequency metrics are applicable.** You must test *Accuracy, Bias, Toxicity,* and *Harmful Content* once as a pre-release baseline benchmark pass. 
  * ❌ **All continuous production metrics** (such as *User Drift, Reliance on GenAI, User Satisfaction,* and *Continuous Verification*) **are NOT applicable** until the code migrates to live environments.

### 📊 Rule 2: The Data Supply Chain Filter (RAG & Ingestion Engines)
* **IF your AI system utilizes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), connects to internal document databases, or fetches corporate text archives:**
  * 🟢 **THEN the following metrics are MANDATORY by default:**
    * `Model Quality: Hallucination` (To check for unverified claims)
    * `Model Quality: Context Precision` (To audit the database chunking engine)
    * `Model Quality: Context Recall` (To verify if the model gets the right background data)

### 👥 Rule 3: The Personal Data & Human Impact Filter
* **IF your AI system processes Customer PII, financial ledgers, or makes decisions that impact human livelihoods (Hiring, Credit Scoring, Loan Approvals, Access Tiers):**
  * 🟢 **THEN the following metrics are MANDATORY by default:**
    * `Safety & Security: Fairness` (Four-Fifths Rule compliance)
    * `Safety & Security: Bias` (To watch for demographic skew)
    * `Human Interaction: Reliance on GenAI` (To detect and mitigate dangerous human automation bias)
    * `Data Privacy: PII Exfiltration` (From the monitoring log schema tracking)

### 🤖 Rule 4: The Interface & Interaction Filter (Chatbots vs. Backends)
* **IF your AI system is an interactive, text-generating customer Chatbot or a Multi-Agent autonomous swarm:**
  * 🟢 **THEN the following metrics are MANDATORY by default:**
    * `Model Quality: Answer Relevance` (To check if the bot answers what was actually asked)
    * `Model Quality: Fluency` (To monitor grammatical and text generation structure drops)
    * `Safety & Security: Toxicity` (To block profane or rogue outbound text generations)
    * `Safety & Security: Harmful Content` (To shield against jailbreaking inputs)
    * `Business Value: User Satisfaction` (To track thumbs up/down and engagement drops)
