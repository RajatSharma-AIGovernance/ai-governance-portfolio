## 🏛️ Module 1: Executive Summary & Objective

Organizations frequently suffer from **"Shadow AI"**—where internal business groups or third-party engineering contractors connect corporate infrastructure to unvetted external AI applications without Information Security, Privacy, or Legal oversight. 

This toolkit establishes a binding, formalized entry gate. It forces business requestors and application architects to comprehensively document the *business intent, data footprint, cloud architecture,* and *operational constraints* of any proposed AI system before line-of-business capital allocation or system profiling approval.

## 📅 Module 2: The Step-by-Step Operational Intake Lifecycle

```text
[ Business Requestor ] ──> Fills Ingestion Questionnaire ──> [ Automated Triage Gate ]
                                                                      │
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┐
  ▼                                                   ▼                                                  ▼

Auto-approved with mandatory                    Requires Information Security                       Requires a formal independent
enterprise baseline guardrails.                 & Privacy Group Sign-Off.                          Algorithmic Impact Assessment (AIA).
```

1. **Identification:** The internal business unit or delivery lead completes and submits the *AI System Procurement & Intake Form* (Module 3).
2. **Triage:** The AI Governance Officer evaluates data types, algorithmic autonomy levels, and jurisdictional boundaries.
3. **Classification:** The application is systematically assigned a formal Risk Tier (Low Risk, Medium Risk, High Risk, or Prohibited) aligned with global frameworks [2.1].
4. **Action Registry:** Upon clearance, the application metadata is securely logged into the centralized *Enterprise AI System Inventory Log* (Module 4) for ongoing continuous validation.

### 🔒 Section C: Data Governance & Jurisdictional Footprint
* **Data Classification Checkpoints (Select all that apply):**
  * [ ] Ingests or processes Customer **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** or protected health identifiers.
  * [ ] Accesses Corporate **Protected Intellectual Property (IP)**, private software code bases, or trade secrets.

### 🤖 Section D: System Autonomy & Human Oversight Hooks
* **Autonomy Configuration Model (Select One):**
  * [ ] **Fully Autonomous (Human-out-of-the-Loop):** The AI agent or model evaluates parameters, calls external API tools, and executes operational decisions or transactions completely on autopilot without human confirmation.
  * [ ] **Human-Assisted (Human-in-the-Loop):** The AI model serves as a recommendation engine. It prints potential insights to a frontend dashboard, but the action remains locked until a verified human employee clicks to approve or reject the final outcome.
  * [ ] **Informational Only:** Used exclusively for internal brainstorming, content drafting, or static text formatting loops with zero operational code footprint.
