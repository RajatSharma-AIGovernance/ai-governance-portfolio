## 🏛️ Module 1: Legal Transparency & Cognitive Fallibility
As regulatory frameworks like the **EU AI Act** mature, compliance gates demand strict transparency regarding data provenance. Under structural transparency obligations, organizations must prove their models do not infringe upon protected data boundaries or violate public copyright opt-outs during training or Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) loops. 

Simultaneously, the systemic risk of **Automation Bias**—the human tendency to blindly trust machine outputs without validation—presents severe legal, operational, and ethical liabilities. This manual establishes the baseline standard to verify both content lineage and cognitive human guardrails.

## ⚖️ Module 2: Content Provenance & Automation Bias Control Matrix

This matrix codifies the required compliance gates to safeguard data lineage integrity and human-in-the-loop operational efficacy.

| Control Code | Governance Overlay Vector | Plain-English Meaning (What it is) | The Enterprise Risk (What breaks) | The Engineering Fix (How to solve) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CP-01** | **Copyright Opt-Out Scoping** | Auditing external text corpora and data sources for explicit reservation of rights statements. | Ingesting copyrighted materials into corporate vector databases triggers multi-million dollar intellectual property lawsuits. | Deploy automated web-crawler parsers that strictly enforce `robots.txt` directives and machine-readable copyright opt-out tags. |
| **CP-02** | **RAG Provenance Attribution** | Forcing generation engines to display exact source-document references for every inference. | An LLM generates a text output using private data but hides the source, making independent legal verification impossible. | Architect vector space metadata tracking that forces backend interfaces to render clear citations alongside text generations. |
| **AB-01** | **Cognitive Friction Design** | Deliberately injecting structural validation steps into frontend software interfaces to disrupt human overreliance. | A human operational specialist clicks "Approve" on a highly flawed AI financial advice generation due to decision fatigue. | Force a mandatory 3-second delay on high-risk approvals, accompanied by an explicit, randomly sorted human validation check. |
| **AB-02** | **Contestability Loop Logs** | Documenting every single event where a human employee overrides an AI recommendation. | The AI steering committee cannot audit system efficacy because there is zero record of when the model failed or succeeded. | Code explicit backend event logging capturing human adjustments, storing text variances inside the non-conformity ledger. |
